    Turkish kitap

      Inherited from Ottoman Turkish كتاب, borrowed from Arabic كِتَاب (kitāb).

      Pronunciation

      • IPA(key): /ciˈtap/, [cʰiˈt̪ʰa̠p]
      • Audio:(file)

      Noun

      kitap (definite accusative kitabı, plural kitaplar or (obsolete) kütüp)

      1. book
        Synonyms: (puristic) betik, (puristic) biti
        Boş zamanlarında kitap okur musun? ― Do you read books in your free time?
