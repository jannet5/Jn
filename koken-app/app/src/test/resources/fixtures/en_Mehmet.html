English

Etymology

From Ottoman Turkish محمد (Mehmed, Mehmet), from Arabic مُحَمَّد (muḥammad). Doublet of Muhammad, Mahomet, and Baphomet.

Proper noun

Mehmet

  1. A male given name from Turkish, variant of Muhammad.
