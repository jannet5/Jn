    Swedish

    Swedish Wikipedia has an article on:
    telefon
    Wikipedia sv

    Etymology

    tele- +‎ -fon

    Pronunciation

    • (Sweden) IPA(key): /tɛlɛˈfoːn/
      • Audio:(file)
    • (Finland) IPA(key): /teleˈfuːn/
    • (Sweden, dialectal) IPA(key): /tɛlɛˈfuːn/

    Noun

    telefon c

    1. a phone, a telephone
      ringa någon med en telefon
      call someone with a telephone
      1. a cell phone, a mobile phone
        Synonyms: mobiltelefon, mobil, (colloquial) lur, (colloquial, somewhat dated) nalle, (humorous, dated) yuppienalle
        Har du sett min telefon?
        Have you seen my phone?
        Har du kollat telefonen idag?
        Have you checked your phone today?
    2. phone, telephone (as a communication medium)
      att svara i telefon
      to answer the phone
      (literally, “to answer in telephone [idiomatic]”)
      Hon svarar inte i telefon
      She doesn't answer the phone
      prata/vara/sitta/stå/etc. i telefon
      be on the phone
      kontakta någon per telefon
      contact someone by phone
      • 1986, Michael B. Tretow, “Telefånen [The telefool [blend of telefonen (“the telephone”) and fånen (“the (silly) fool”)]”, in Makalösa manickar [more commonly "manicker"] [Incredible contraptions]‎[5]:
        Ja, jag står i telefon ute på gatan utanför. Jag kan se din siluett genom gardin[en]. Och jag står förstås och drömmer som jag nästan alltid gör när jag har satt i mig några flaskor vin.
        Yes, I'm on the phone [stand on the phone] out on the street outside. I can see your silhouette through the curtain. And I'm standing here dreaming, of course [and I stand of course and dream], like I almost always do when I've downed a few bottles of wine.
    3. short for telefonsamtal (“phone call”)
      Du har telefon
      You have a phone call

    Usage notes

    • In some dialects, this word is not conjugated in the definitive, hence "svara i telefon" is equivalent to "answer the phone" rather than the ungrammatical "answer phone".
