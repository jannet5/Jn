Dutch

Etymology

From French cadre, from Italian quadro (“framed painting, square”), from Latin quadrum, from quattuor (“four”).

Pronunciation

  • Audio:(file)
  • Rhymes: -aːdər

Noun

kader n (plural kaders, diminutive kadertje n)

  1. frame
  2. (by extension) cadre, framework
    Synonym: raamwerk
  3. (politics, particularly socialism) cadre, member of the core group of a political organization
    Synonym: kaderlid
    de gestaalde kaders van de communistische partij ― the steeled cadres of the communist party
  4. (politics, particularly socialism) the cadres of a political organization, collectively
    het kader stond achter de leider ― the core members supported the leader

Derived terms

  • in het kader van
  • kaderen
  • kaderlid
  • partijkader
  • referentiekader
  • toetsingskader

Indonesian

Etymology

From Dutch kader, from French cadre, from Italian quadro (“framed painting, square”), from Latin quadrum, from quattuor (“four”).

Pronunciation

  • IPA(key): [ˈkadər]
  • Hyphenation: ka‧dêr

Noun

kadêr (plural kader-kader)

  1. cadre
    1. the core of a managing group, or a member of such a group
    2. a small group of people specially trained for a particular purpose or profession

Derived terms

  • mengader
  • pengader
  • pengaderan

Related terms

  • kaderisasi

Slovak

Etymology

    Inherited from Proto-Slavic *kǫdrь.

    Pronunciation

    • IPA(key): /kaɟer/, [ˈkaɟer]
    • Rhymes: -aɟer
    • Hyphenation: ka‧der

    Noun

    kader f

    1. (usually in the plural, poetic) hair
      Synonym: vlas
    2. (usually in the plural, dated) curl (lock of curling hair)
      Synonym: kučera
