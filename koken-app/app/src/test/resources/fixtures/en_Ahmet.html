Serbo-Croatian

Etymology

Borrowed from Ottoman Turkish احمد (ahmed), from Arabic أَحْمَد (ʔaḥmad).

Pronunciation

  • IPA(key): /axmet/

Proper noun

Ahmet m anim (Cyrillic spelling Ахмет)

  1. (chiefly Bosnia) a male given name from Arabic, equivalent to English Ahmad
