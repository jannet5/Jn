Arabic

Root
م ي ز (m y z)
14 terms

Etymology

    Derived from the active participle of the verb اِمْتَازَ (imtāza, “to be distinguished”).

    Pronunciation

    • IPA(key): /mum.taːz/
    • Rhymes: -aːz

    Adjective

    مُمْتَاز • (mumtāz) (feminine مُمْتَازَة (mumtāza), masculine plural مُمْتَازُون (mumtāzūn), feminine plural مُمْتَازَات (mumtāzāt))

    1. excellent
    2. distinguished, selected
    3. privileged

    Persian

    Etymology

      Borrowed from Arabic مُمْتَاز (mumtāz).

      Pronunciation

      • (Classical Persian) IPA(key): /mum.ˈtaːz/
       
      • (Dari, formal) IPA(key): [mʊm.t̪ʰɑ́ːz]
      • (Dari, formal) IPA(key): [mʊm.t̪ʰɑ́ːz]
        • (Kabuli) IPA(key): [mʊm.t̪ʰɑ́ːz]
        • (Hazaragi) IPA(key): [mum.t̪ʰɔ́ːz]
      • (Iran, formal) IPA(key): [mom.t̪ʰɒ́ːz]
      • (Tajik, formal) IPA(key): [mum.t̪ʰɔ́z]
      • Rhymes: -aːz

      Readings
      Classical reading? mumtāz
      Dari reading? mumtāz
      Iranian reading? momtâz
      Tajik reading? mumtoz

      Adjective

      ممتاز • (mumtāz / momtâz) (Tajik spelling мумтоз)

      1. excellent, distinguished, select
        Synonyms: برجسته (barjaste), عالی (âli)

      Descendants

      • → Bengali: মমতাজ (momotaj)

        • Hindi: मुमताज़
        • Urdu: ممتاز
      • → Gujarati: મુમતાજ (mumtāj)
