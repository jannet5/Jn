    Pronunciation

    • IPA(key): /im.taː.za/

    Verb

    اِمْتَازَ • (imtāza) VIII (non-past يَمْتَازُ (yamtāzu), verbal noun اِمْتِيَاز (imtiyāz))

    1. to be characterized or distinguished by
    2. to surpass, to be superior
