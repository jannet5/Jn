Azerbaijani

Etymology

    Borrowed from Arabic مُمْتَاز (mumtāz).

    Adjective

    mümtaz (comparative daha mümtaz, superlative ən mümtaz)

    1. (archaic) outstanding, distinguished, remarkable
      Synonyms: seçilən, seçilmiş
    2. (archaic) excellent
      Synonym: əla
    3. (archaic) privileged
      Synonym: imtiyazlı

    References

    • Orucov, Əliheydər, editor (2006), “mümtaz”, in Azərbaycan dilinin izahlı lüğəti [Explanatory Dictionary of the Azerbaijani Language]‎[1] (in Azerbaijani), 2nd edition, volume 3, Baku: Şərq-Qərb

    Turkish mümtaz

      Inherited from Ottoman Turkish ممتاز, from Arabic مُمْتَاز (mumtāz).

      Adjective

      mümtaz(archaic)

      1. distinguished, selected
        Synonym: seçkin
      2. excellent
      3. privileged; autonomous
