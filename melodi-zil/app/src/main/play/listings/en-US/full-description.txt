Melodi Zil doesn't cut a song, it turns it into a melody. Lyrics and vocals disappear; a clean, recognizable tune remains, like classic phone ringtones.

1. Pick an audio file from your phone or paste a video link under 10 minutes.
2. The melody is extracted on your phone; nothing is uploaded.
3. Choose one of 8 forms: Piano, Music Box, 8-Bit, Marimba, Flute, Bell, Guitar, Synth.
4. "Auto" finds the busiest section (usually the chorus), or drag to pick your own.
5. Save as ringtone, notification or alarm in one tap.

No ads, no accounts, no subscriptions. Please only use content you have the rights to.
