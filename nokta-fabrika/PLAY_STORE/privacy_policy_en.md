# nokta Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

nokta is a list app. This policy explains what data it handles.

## Data we collect
**None.** nokta has no accounts, sign-in, ads, analytics or tracking. The items you type are stored only in the app's storage on your device and are never sent off the device. The app does not request the internet permission.

## Sharing
Your data is not shared with third parties. When you tap "Share", you send the list text to the app you pick through Android's share sheet. That action is under your control.

## Permissions
- **Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW):** to show the floating bubble and the list panel that opens from it. It runs only if you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time.
- **Foreground service (special use) and notification:** to show the silent notification Android requires while the bubble is on.

## Deleting data
Uninstalling the app removes all list data from the device. In the app you can delete items one by one or clear completed items.

## Children
The app collects no personal data, including from children.
