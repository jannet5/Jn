                          AI Receptionist
                         Training Checklist
 A complete framework for onboarding, configuring, and deploying an AI receptionist that
                          sounds competent — not clueless.




LAYER 1

Business Identity
Establish the foundation before training anything else. Without a clear identity, every response sounds
generic.


  Business name, physical location, and full service area

  Hours of operation — including holiday closures and seasonal schedule changes

  Brand voice and tone guidelines (formal, friendly, concise, etc.)

  Key differentiators — what sets this business apart from local competitors



LAYER 2

Services & Pricing
The AI must know exactly what is — and is not — offered. A single wrong answer erodes trust
immediately.


  Complete service list with clear, plain-language descriptions

  Pricing structure — fixed rates, tiered packages, or ‘we provide custom quotes’

  Current promotions, seasonal offers, or new-client specials

  Services the business does NOT offer — be explicit (e.g., a pest control company that does not handle bee
  removal must state that clearly)



LAYER 3

The FAQ Library
 The depth of your knowledge base determines whether the AI sounds competent or clueless. Gather
 answers before you build anything.

Discovery Questions to Ask the Business Owner

     ■ What are the 20 questions your receptionist answers every single day?

     ■ What questions make you cringe when customers ask?

     ■ What is the one thing people always get wrong about your business?

     ■ What complaints do you receive that could be prevented with better upfront information?

FAQ Checklist Items

   Compile top 20 frequently asked questions from staff input and call logs

   Document accurate, approved answers for each FAQ

   Identify 'cringe' questions — and craft calm, professional responses

   Address common misconceptions about the business or its services

   List preventable complaints and the information that would prevent them



 LAYER 4

 Calendar & Booking Logic
 Real-time calendar access transforms the AI from an information tool into a revenue-generating booking
 engine.


   Confirm whether the AI is authorised to book appointments directly

   Connect to the business's calendar system (Google Calendar, Outlook, etc.)

   Define all appointment types and their plain-language names

   Set duration for each appointment type

   Identify which team members or resources can be booked

   Establish routing rules — does the AI assign a specific technician, room, or staff member?

   Configure buffer times, lead times, and any blackout periods


     ■ Why It Matters

     When AI receptionists have real-time calendar access, they complete bookings in under 60 seconds.
     Businesses typically see 30% more appointments simply by removing the 'we’ll call you back' delay.
LAYER 5

No-Show & Follow-Up Automation
This is where you become indispensable to the business. Automated touchpoints protect revenue and
build client loyalty.


  Automated confirmation message sent immediately after every booking

  24-hour reminder — text or email based on client preference

  Same-day reminder sent 2–4 hours before the appointment

  No-show detection logic with automatic rebooking outreach sequence

  Post-appointment follow-up requesting a review or referral

  Opt-out mechanism included in all automated messages (compliance)


    ■ Industry Benchmark

    Automated appointment reminders reduce no-shows by 30–45%. For a business running 100
    appointments per month, that translates directly to recovered revenue — often thousands of dollars
    monthly.




LAYER 6

Escalation Rules
Local businesses worry about AI errors and data privacy. Defining clear human handoff rules upfront
builds trust — and separates professional agencies from amateur setups.


    ■■ Address This Before Deployment

    Agencies that do not proactively address escalation and human handoff lose credibility with business
    owners. Bring this up first — it signals professionalism and puts clients at ease.



  Define clear conditions for transferring the conversation to a human agent

  Configure urgent-trigger keywords: ‘emergency,’ ‘complaint,’ ‘cancel,’ ‘lawyer,’ etc.

  Set after-hours escalation path — who receives urgent notifications and how

  Establish data privacy boundaries — what the AI may and may not store or share

  Document fallback messaging when the AI cannot confidently answer a question

  Confirm the human handoff experience is seamless (full context passed, no repeat questions)

  Test edge cases: angry callers, out-of-scope requests, technical failures
