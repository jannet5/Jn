Welcome to the first episode of my new series, Vibe Coding.

In this series, I recreate Awwwards-level websites using AI tools as my coding partner. For Episode 1, I rebuilt the Services Card Section from the award-winning website Truus.co (https://truus.co/), which was featured on Awwwards as Site of the Day.

Instead of manually coding everything from scratch, I collaborated with Google Antigravity and TRAE AI throughout the process. I carefully studied the original layout, animations, hover effects, spacing, and interaction details. After analyzing the design, I broke everything down into structured prompts and guided the AI step by step, refining the generated output until it matched the original experience as closely as possible.

This is not just about cloning a website. It is about reverse engineering premium UI, understanding animation logic, improving prompt engineering skills, and learning how to collaborate effectively with AI to build high-quality frontend experiences.

In this video, you will see how I analyze an Awwwards website, structure prompts for better AI results, and recreate smooth services card interactions while explaining my complete thought process during development.

This marks the beginning of my journey to master high-level frontend development through AI collaboration. More sections from this website will be recreated in upcoming episodes as part of the Vibe Coding series.

If you are interested in creative frontend development, modern UI/UX, and AI-assisted web building, this series is for you.
