If you’re running Facebook ads for your restaurant, are you giving customers the right call to action?

In this restaurant marketing audit, I break down Little Caesars’ Facebook ads and show how restaurants can use Facebook advertising to generate more than just immediate orders.

One of the biggest mistakes restaurants make with Facebook ads is sending everyone straight to an “Order Now” page. The problem? Most people who see your ad aren’t ready to order at that exact moment.

Instead, your Facebook ad strategy can give customers another reason to take action:

• Join your loyalty program
• Download your restaurant app
• Claim a special offer
• Join your email or SMS list
• Get an incentive they can redeem later

I also show you how to use the Meta Ad Library to research your competitors’ Facebook ads, see what they’re promoting and find ads they’ve been running for weeks or months.
