A skill is a reusable set of instructions and context that any AI Agent like Claude Code or Codex loads on its own. Unlike a .md file you have to paste in every time, a skill is already programmed: it reads its own description, sees that your prompt matches, and applies it automatically giving you a standardized output every time.

The problem is that most of us keep re-teaching AI the same patterns over and over, especially the repeatable parts of a design system: component conventions, tokens, handoff. This video is about what a skill actually is, how to use one, and how to wire skills into your design system so your whole team can move faster.
