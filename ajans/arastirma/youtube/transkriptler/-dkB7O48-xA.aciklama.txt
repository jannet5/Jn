In this video, I show you how I designed and built a fully animated hero section for a website in less than 90 minutes — using Claude Code together with AI-generated visuals from Higgsfield. The hero section uses a video scrubbing effect, where the video only plays as you scroll, transitioning from a smaller framed shot into a full-screen interior scene with a large heading.

I walk you through the full workflow: generating the start and end frames with Nano Banana, creating the video with Seedance 2.0, testing the new Claude Design app and why I eventually switched back to Claude Code, and building the hero section step by step. I also show how I reversed the entire transition using Git branches, and added a polished loading animation as a finishing touch.
