A lot of teams have the same problem with their design system. It's old, components live in 10,000 different places. It's not organized, outdated. It's just a mess. So the question becomes, do you put in the time and effort to rebuild based on what you have, or do you just redo it?

My suggestion is don't redo it from scratch. You can easily redo it with shadcn. It's already built in a way AI understands, it's agentic, and you can completely customize it with your brand.

In this week's video, I walk through the full migration. We start by scaffolding shadcn as a sibling package in an existing codebase so nothing in production breaks. Then we layer in design.md, agents.md, and the meta.ts pattern that stops AI from making up components. And we end on how to actually bring this to your engineering team without getting blocked.
