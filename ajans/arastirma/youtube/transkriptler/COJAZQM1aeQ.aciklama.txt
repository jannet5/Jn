Nate, a designer at Anthropic, uses Claude design in Claude Code to take his side project's onboarding from rough idea to working prototype.

He connects his repo, opens the Artifacts tab, and asks Claude for design options. Then leaves comments on what's working, fixes details himself in the editor, and asks for a click-through prototype before telling Claude to build it. A quick design process like this fits anywhere you'd normally skip straight to code.
