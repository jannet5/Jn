**App name (max 30):**
WinRemote Monitor

**Short description (max 80):**
Watch your Windows PC from your phone: CPU, RAM, disks, apps and file changes.

**Full description (max 4000):**

What is your Windows PC doing? Check from your phone.

WinRemote Monitor lets you securely monitor and lightly manage your own Windows PC from an Android phone on the same Wi-Fi network.

SEE
• Whether the PC is online
• Live CPU, memory and disk usage
• Running programs and which one is slowing the PC down
• Files created, grown, moved or deleted in Downloads, Desktop and Documents
• "What is filling my disk": fastest-growing folders in the last hour, day and week

MANAGE
• Close a frozen program with one tap (critical Windows processes are protected)
• Start programs you have explicitly allowed
• See a history of every action taken from the phone

GET ALERTS
• Disk almost full
• A very large file appeared
• A folder is growing fast

SECURE
• Encrypted connection; only the phone you paired by QR code can connect
• The phone cannot run commands on the PC and never reads file contents (only names, sizes and locations)
• Your data never goes to our servers: the phone talks to your PC directly
• No ads, no account

SET UP IN 2 MINUTES
1. Open this app and tap "Send link" to send yourself the Windows download link.
2. On the PC, open the link and run the WinRemote Monitor installer (Next, Next, Finish).
3. Scan the QR code shown on the PC with your phone. Done.

Requirements: a Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) PC; phone and PC on the same Wi-Fi network.
